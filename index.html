Our Story

Grilled with fire and patience in Azam Basti.

Waheed B.B.Q is a local grill house built around one simple idea — good BBQ takes time. Our meats are marinated with house spice blends, skewered fresh, and cooked low over live charcoal until every bite carries real smoke and depth of flavour.

From quick evening plates to full event spreads, we grill for the neighbourhood and for the occasions that bring people together.
